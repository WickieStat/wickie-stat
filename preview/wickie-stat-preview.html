Academic integrity & ethical boundaries

The objective is to help researchers understand their data, improve their analytical methods and develop independent research capabilities.

We do provide

  • Research methodology consultations
  • Statistical analysis guidance
  • Data cleaning and preparation
  • Questionnaire design guidance
  • Statistical interpretation
  • Python, R, SPSS and Stata programming support
  • Guidance on research findings and presentation
  • Technical support for reproducible research
  • Feedback on analytical methods and research outputs

We do not

  • Ghost-write entire Master's or PhD theses for students to submit as their own work.
  • Write academic assignments or examinations for submission under another person's name.
  • Fabricate research findings, datasets or statistical results.
  • Manipulate statistical outputs to produce predetermined conclusions.
  • Invent references, citations or research sources.
  • Guarantee academic grades, research approval or publication acceptance.
  • Participate in academic examination cheating.
  • Guarantee financial returns from Forex trading or investments.
  • Promise that any trading strategy will always be profitable.
  • Fabricate business performance statistics.
  • Misrepresent qualifications or professional experience.
  • Undertake projects involving illegal access to computer systems or unauthorised data.
  • Share confidential client information without permission.

Common questions

What is Wickie Stat Consultancy?

A professional consultancy providing data science, research analytics, statistical analysis, programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning and financial engineering services to individuals, researchers, businesses, startups and organisations.

Who can use your services?

Undergraduate and postgraduate researchers, Master's and PhD researchers, universities and research institutions, businesses and startups, NGOs and development organisations, entrepreneurs, professionals needing data-driven solutions, and international clients who need remote consultancy.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We offer remote consultancy to clients in Kenya, the United Kingdom, the United States, Sweden and other international markets.

Do you provide online consultations?

Yes. Consultations can be arranged through WhatsApp, email or online meetings, depending on your preference and the project's requirements.

How long does a project take?

It depends on complexity, scope and data availability. Indicative timelines: basic statistical analysis 2–5 working days; research data analysis 3–10 working days; Python/R programming 3–14 working days; machine learning 1–4 weeks; interactive dashboards 3–14 working days; complex AI applications after a technical assessment. These are estimates, not guaranteed deadlines — a confirmed timeline follows a review of your project.

Can you handle urgent projects?

Yes, subject to availability and complexity. Urgent requests are assessed individually and may attract additional charges.

How much do your services cost?

There's no single fixed price. Pricing depends on project complexity, the amount of data, technical requirements, deliverables, timeline and the level of consultancy needed. You always receive a quotation before any work begins.

Do you require a deposit?

For larger projects, an agreed initial deposit may be required before work starts. Payment milestones are set according to the project scope.

Which payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa for eligible Kenyan clients, bank transfers, international bank transfers, and other mutually agreed electronic payment methods. Exact payment instructions are shared directly when the project is confirmed.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Reasonable revisions are available where deliverables don't meet the originally agreed specifications — typically up to two revision rounds for eligible projects. Requests that substantially change the original scope may need a separate quotation.

How do you ensure quality?

Projects follow an agreed workflow: requirements assessment, data validation, appropriate analytical methods, quality checks and review of final deliverables. Where applicable, you also receive explanations of the methodology, procedures and interpretation of findings.

Is my research data confidential?

Yes. Client information, datasets, research materials and project discussions are treated as confidential and are never publicly disclosed without permission.

Can I share sensitive research datasets?

Please only share information you're authorised to disclose, and remove or anonymise personal identifiers wherever possible. Project files are handled with care and not used for unrelated purposes.

Do you publish client projects in your portfolio?

Only with the client's explicit permission. Client confidentiality takes priority over portfolio promotion.
